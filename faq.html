Yes, absolutely. RUX Multi Surface Cleaner has a carefully buffered neutral pH formula that does not etch, pit, or scratch natural marble, granite, or ceramic tile glazes. It leaves surfaces sparkling without a sticky residue.

What is RUX Solar Panel Cleaner, and why is it needed?

Regular tap water leaves mineral calcium spots that bake onto solar glass and reduce sunlight absorption. RUX Solar Cleaner has an anti-static, de-ionizing formula that breaks down soot, dust, and bird droppings, restoring photovoltaic efficiency by up to 25% without damaging the anti-reflective coating.
